LifeHub for Mac
===============

1. Drag LifeHub onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click LifeHub.

The first time, macOS says LifeHub "can't be opened" or is from an
"unidentified developer" (it isn't notarised by Apple yet). To allow it once:

   System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → next to
   "LifeHub was blocked…" press "Open Anyway", then "Open".

   (Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LifeHub.app)

LifeHub then opens its setup assistant and asks how you want to use it:
just this Mac, this Mac + your phone, a household server, Docker or
Home Assistant. It can also install the free AI (Ollama) for you.

Full manual: https://github.com/shotgn16/LifeHub/tree/main/docs/manual
